Project Name: Rise Again

Project Type:
AI powered career comeback guidance platform (Mobile + Web App)

Problem:
Many people take long career or education breaks due to family responsibility,
financial issues, health problems, maternity, mental stress or personal situations.
After the break they feel confused, lack confidence and don’t know where to restart.
They don’t know what skills are outdated, what to learn now, and how to reach a job again.

Solution:
Rise Again is an AI mentor system that analyzes the user’s past education,
experience, gap duration and current interest. Then it creates a personalized
step-by-step comeback roadmap from current state to dream job.

Core Features:
1. User profile analysis (education, skills, gap reason, duration)
2. AI career path prediction
3. Personalized learning roadmap
4. Daily/weekly task guidance
5. Skill gap detection
6. Practice tests & interview questions
7. Confidence rebuilding support
8. Mentor style chatbot guidance
9. Job readiness score
10. Suggestion of companies to apply

End Goal:
Make users job-ready and help them confidently attend interviews in
companies like product companies and MNCs.

Technology:
Frontend: Web/Mobile App
Backend: Cloud based services
AI: Recommendation and analysis engine
Cloud: AWS services for storage, compute and AI processing

Target Users:
Students and professionals restarting career after a long gap

Expected Outcome:
Reduce career restart confusion
Provide clear roadmap
Improve employability
Increase successful job placements
